Stay awake on your terms

For downloads, renders, presentations, long builds, or just reading without the screen going dark.

☕

Indefinitely

One click on the cup and the machine stays up until you click again.

⏱

Never touches your power plan

Cafe doesn't change any power settings. It asks the OS for a temporary request instead: SetThreadExecutionState on Windows, an IOKit power assertion on macOS.

Switch it off, quit it, or let it crash, and the request ends with it. The machine goes straight back to the plan you already had, so there's nothing to restore.

You can check it yourself with powercfg /requests or pmset -g assertions, where it shows up under its own name.
